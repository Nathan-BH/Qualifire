## 1. The design in one paragraph

The data model already knows loops (a Route whose start and end landmark are the same, with a `loopDiscriminator`; the naming card and catalog screens handle them) and the live engine's chainage is forward-only, which is exactly what makes a retraced street unambiguous *once the rider is anchored on the right pass*. Four things are missing. (1) The picker cannot express "finish where I start": GOING TO hides the START place. Brief 01 adds a `loop` pill — a UI sentinel `'~loop'` that resolves to the START place at the moment of use (route lookup, armed title, start context), so it follows a START change and the old `to === fromId` hidden state becomes the same visible `loop` selection. (2) Every place the engine picks a reference VERTEX without a chainage context — the first fix, re-acquisition after a signal loss, and the same two spots in the offline projector that stored results, sector rows, self dots and replay all use — takes "geometrically nearest", which on retraced ground is a coin toss between passes; on a loop it lands at chainage ≈ L and FINISH fires `estimated` at t = 0 (confirmed by running the current core: first fix 4 m north of Home → `s = 3195`, `[{gateIndex: 4, estimated: true}]`, gates 0-3 skipped). Brief 02 adds one rule in `core/src/projection.ts` — `passVertex`: split the candidate vertices into passes (> 120 m apart in chainage), take the pass nearest the chainage the rider already has, or the earliest pass when there is none — and uses it at those four sites. Forward-only projection then self-corrects a too-early guess and could never undo a too-late one; a single-pass line is unchanged by construction. (3) Gate seeding and the stop chainages that steer it know nothing about retraced ground: brief 03 makes the seeder slide a sector gate off retraced ground (same ±250 m window as the stop rule) and projects the reference ride's stops through the (now pass-aware) offline projector in ride order instead of a global nearest-segment search. (4) The map draws one polyline and name-keyed gate ticks: brief 04 gives `WayMapView` one optional `progressM` input (engine chainage live, recorded chainage in REPLAY, a path-metres progress in DEMO), a per-vertex pass model computed once per asset, and `line-opacity` expressions so the copy of a retraced stretch — and its gates and coloured sector spans — that the rider is NOT currently on draws at 0.3 until the rider's chainage comes within the engine's own 240 m look-ahead of it; it also makes the gate-editor tap cycle through stacked gates instead of taking `features[0]`.

## 5. Risks and limits

1. **Parity `[UNVERIFIED]`** (D6): `projectRideOffline`'s anchor/re-acq go through `passVertex`. Identical for single-pass lines by construction; `engine_suite.ts` passing unedited is the evidence on this branch.
2. **Window ambiguity under 240 m** (D5): a tour that returns onto itself with < 240 m (live) / < 300 m (offline) of chainage between passes can snap forward inside the window. Not handled.
3. **Live re-acquisition after a long GPS gap on retraced ground**: the time-aware bound can span both passes; `nearS = sp` picks the earlier one — right if the rider is still outbound, wrong if they already turned; forward-only then stalls until the rider reaches the return copy, gates in between fire `estimated` (jump > 100 m). Degrades to estimated, never to a wrong finish.
4. **The fade is per-vertex and the two copies often share pixels** at the live zoom: dimming the other copy of the LINE is mostly invisible when the copies coincide (bright on top of faint ≈ bright) and visible when they run a few metres apart or when the done copy is coloured. The GATE ticks are where the rider will see it. Accepted; Nathan judges on device.
5. **`wayMapView.tsx`** (D11, D12) is a 1 000-line mount-order-sensitive file: brief 04 confines itself to the props type, one memo, three paint blocks, one handler and one import; `git diff` is pinned in its acceptance list; the two static mount-order tests must pass.
6. **Stop chainage change (D7) affects every NEW reference** (not only retraced ones): for a single-pass reference the offline projection of the ride's own smoothed line is the same point the global nearest-segment search found (the ride is on its own corridor), so seeds should be unchanged; brief 03 pins this with `northRide` producing the same `stopChainageM` before and after.
7. **On-device only**: pill wrapping on narrow phones (one more pill in GOING TO), opacity 0.3 legibility on the day and night basemaps. Nathan to eyeball.
