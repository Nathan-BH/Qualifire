## Q1 — Should the window-best self be visually singled out (purple), or should all selfs look identical?

Brief's default: the fastest self of the window renders purple ("the one to beat"), the
others muted/translucent. Reason: with nine dots you race the pack *and* one target, and
purple already means "beats the best of the window" everywhere else in the app. The
alternative — nine identical dots — is calmer and avoids implying a target on the D-006
no-controls surface.

## Q2 — Do you want any text at all — a gap readout to the purple self, or an off-screen "ahead/behind" edge marker?

Brief's default: **none**. Dots only, per your original ask, and because the live pane's
standing rule is "no target/benchmark/delta anywhere near the clock". The live map is a
tight heading-up crop, so a self far ahead or behind will simply be off-screen. If that
turns out to be frustrating in practice, an edge marker (a small arrow at the screen edge
with "+0:12") is a contained follow-up.

## Q3 — When SETTINGS → Timing is on *moving* time, should selfs pause while you are stopped?

Brief's default: **no** — selfs always move on raw wall-clock elapsed since the START gate,
in both modes; only *which* self is purple follows the active mode. A dot is where past-you
physically was. Pausing nine dots at every red light is a different feature and would make
the dots disagree with the map (a self stopped at a light you sailed through would
teleport). Only worth revisiting if you actually ride in moving-time mode.
