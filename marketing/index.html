How it works

Same road. New meaning.

Press START at the door. The lap doesn't begin there — it begins about 162 metres later, at a line only the app can see. You're already at speed when it happens. That's the flying start.

1

Ride your normal route

Morning, Evening A, Evening B — three known tracks. Nothing about the ride changes. The GPS trace is the same one your phone always drew.

2

Invisible gates split it into sectors

Lines drawn perpendicular across your path, downstream of junctions, never at stop lines. You never see them. You only feel the moment one falls behind you.

3

Every sector is timed against your last 10

Not a personal record from some mythical perfect ride — the rolling window of your last ten laps on that exact stretch. Recent you, not peak you.

While you ride — race mode

The screen drops to black. The colours do the talking.

The moment you press START, the app leaves paddock charcoal behind for near-black. Chrome recedes. Only tier colour and tabular numerals remain — built for a glance under a second, not a stare.

Earned colour

Colour is the scarcest resource in the app.

Every sector is measured against a comparison window: your last 10 rides on that exact track.

Purple

Beats the best of the window. The fastest this sector has ever gone in your last ten attempts. Rare on purpose — that's what makes it worth chasing.

Green

Above your recent average. A genuinely good lap — better than what you've been doing lately, without needing to be a lifetime best.

Yellow

Below your recent average — styled as "time posted," not failure. This is the app's default temperature. Most laps are yellow. That's fine.

Colour only fires beyond a measured noise floor — a colour can never be luck-of-the-GPS. Purple is rare by design. Fewer than five comparable rides on a track and the sector simply stays as plain ink — no colour at all — waiting for history to accumulate.

QUALIFIRE

Qualifire is a personal project. One rider, three tracks, zero servers. It runs off a single archive of one person's own commute data and exists to make an ordinary ride worth riding again — nothing more.

Not available on any app store. Not a company. Not for sale. This page is a mock, built to show what the app looks and feels like, not to sell it to anyone but its one user.
