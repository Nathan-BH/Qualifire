## 2. Getting user feedback

## 3. Global or local release

- Should I go for a global kind of release, or focus locally where I live / the university / Leuven itself, and start marketing there?

## 4. The tension I feel about local vs distribution

- I like the local idea: sharing the app first with colleagues, friends and more, without an immediate global launch.
- The issue is getting the app to them. I cannot send them the raw .apk file, which triggers a lot of phone warnings. I need a proper distribution channel like the Play Store.
- But then I think it is already "out there" too much?

## 5. Protecting my idea and intellectual property

- I have a lot of questions about how to protect my idea and project intellectual property.
- I would be open to working with app designers or other people who might help me, but what if they run with the idea? Should I protect it first?
- I think protecting things like logos, the app and ideas is expensive, which might not be worth it if this project amounts to nothing. So it is a bit of a chicken-and-egg story for me.
