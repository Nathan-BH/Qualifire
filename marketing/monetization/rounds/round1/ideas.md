# Monetization: Round 1 ideas (Nathan)

Date: 2026-10-04
Status: raw ideas, first brain dump. Nathan's ideas only, no review (the review goes in a separate file).

---

## 1. Make the app paid

Two price directions:

- **Really cheap (around 1 euro).** Keeps entry affordable, and the hope is that it catches on so the volume makes it worth it.
- **More expensive.** Could work if the app is seen through a different lens: a specialized app for people who are really looking for this kind of thing. Concern: it could hinder growth.

Payment model:

- A **one-time payment** means people own the app and are not stuck in a subscription. Nathan personally dislikes subscriptions and does not want to impose one on the world.
- Aware that a subscription is, these days, the preferred way to keep cash flowing in.

Extra work that comes with a paid app:

- A secure payment system.
- Deciding where the money goes.
- Taxes and similar obligations, which is not trivial.

---

## 2. Some kind of partnership

Where the idea came from (test user feedback):

- A test user used the app for a week without a bike phone holder. She would start the app, put the phone in a pocket and just stop the ride at the end.
- She then bought a phone holder for the bike handlebar and said she enjoyed the app way more, because she could finally enjoy the live racing features.

The idea: a mutually beneficial partnership with a brand or retailer of sport equipment, specifically phone holders for car, run and bike, in all shapes and sizes.

Ways it could work:

- **App sponsor.** The company pays a fixed amount (maybe per month or per year) to be the official sponsor of the app. In exchange, its logo or buy link is shown in the app.
- **Referral deal.** I get a percentage for everyone who buys through my link. Users should get a discount through the link so it is worth it for everyone.
- **My own online store.** I sell the holders myself, keep all the money, and the app stays ad free, with the store as an optional extra that makes the experience better. Downsides: a lot of work, and probably competitive.

## 3. Ads in the app

Not really considered before.

- Feeling: ads would take away from the experience.
- Open question: are there ways to place ads that would not hinder the experience?
- It still seems like real work: finding people who want their ad in the app, and figuring out how the monetization works.
