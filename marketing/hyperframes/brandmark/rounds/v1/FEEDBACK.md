## What it's for
A five-second branded sting: the Qualifire "Q" mark builds itself out of a drawn ring and a
dropped-in gold slash, the QUALIFIRE wordmark fades in beneath it, everything holds, then
fades out. It is meant to bookend the other videos (intro/outro) and to double as an animated
avatar/icon. As rendered it is a single centred mark on a near-black field with no camera
motion, no background texture and no supporting graphics — it reads as a clean logo reveal,
nothing more.

## What you see, in order
1. **0.000–1.067 s — Ring draws.** The frame opens on a flat near-black background
   (#070909, not pure black — it stays this shade for the whole file). On the very first
   frame there is already a small off-white dot (~23 px, warm white #F1F1E8) at the
   12 o'clock position of where the ring will be, at roughly x 960 / y 418. From there a
   ~23 px-thick round-capped stroke draws clockwise: it has reached about 2 o'clock by
   0.25 s, 5 o'clock by 0.50 s, 9–10 o'clock by 0.75 s, and the gap at the top closes at
   about 1.07 s. The draw eases out — the last ~10% of the circumference takes the final
   0.15 s or so. Finished ring: ~207 px outer diameter, centred at x 960 / y 510, i.e. its
   centre sits ~30 px above the frame's vertical centre. Nothing else is on screen.
2. **1.067–1.333 s — Ring holds alone.** ~0.27 s of the completed white ring with nothing
   happening.
3. **1.333–1.433 s — Slash lands.** A gold (#EFC43A-ish, warm yellow) round-capped stroke
   snaps in at 45°, from just right-and-below the ring's centre (≈ x 976 / y 526) down-right
   to ≈ x 1073 / y 623, so it crosses the ring's lower-right rim and pokes ~55 px outside it —
   the tail of the "Q". It is essentially a pop: barely visible on the first frame (1.333 s),
   ~90% of its final size and still slightly translucent one frame later (1.367 s), fully
   opaque and settled by 1.433 s. So the whole slash arrival is 3 frames / 0.1 s. Where the
   slash crosses the ring it sits on top of the white stroke.
4. **1.433–2.933 s — Q mark holds.** 1.5 s of the completed ring + slash on black, static.
   This is the longest hold in the piece, and nothing moves during it.
5. **2.933–3.233 s — QUALIFIRE fades in.** The wordmark appears below the mark: "QUALIFIRE"
   in a bold geometric sans, all caps, widely letter-spaced, in the same gold as the slash
   (~#F1C23E). It fades from nothing to full over ~0.3 s (first faint frame at 2.933 s, full
   brightness by ~3.23 s) with a very small upward drift of roughly 6 px during the fade —
   subtle enough that it reads as a plain fade at normal speed. Final geometry: ~626 px wide
   × 66 px cap height, baseline at y 745, top of the letters ~65 px below the bottom of the
   ring. Its visual centre is at x ≈ 947, i.e. ~13 px left of the ring's centre (x 960) —
   almost certainly the trailing letter-spacing after the final "E" pulling the box right
   while the glyphs sit left. Whole lock-up (ring top to wordmark baseline) is 407–745 px
   tall, so the mark occupies only ~31% of the frame height with a lot of empty black
   around it.
6. **3.233–4.533 s — Full lock-up holds.** 1.3 s completely static: white ring, gold slash,
   gold wordmark, black field.
7. **4.533–5.000 s — Fade out.** Ring, slash and wordmark all fade together (the background
   does not change, since it is already the black field). Peak brightness drops steadily
   from 4.533 s; the ring is effectively gone by ~4.9 s. Note this is a **fade, not a cut**,
   and it does not quite finish: on the final frame (4.967 s) the mark is still faintly
   visible at roughly 15–20% — the fade reaches zero only at/after the file's end. If this
   is spliced against another clip the last frame will carry a ghost of the logo.
