# brandmark/opening — lockup variant: draw-in, then wordmark + tagline cross-fade

One of the brandmark lockup variants (see ../README.md for the family and the
survey of variants). This is the teaser's opening (position 1 of ../../teaser/).

wordmark and the tagline "Same road. New meaning." cross-fade IN, overlapping.
