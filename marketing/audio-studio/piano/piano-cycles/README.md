# piano-cycles

Lightweight log of small, iterative tweaks to `../named-keys.html` (the "Named Keys"
player) — the kind of change that's too small/UI-focused to belong in the main
Qualifire app's `cycles/` pipeline, and not scoped to one specific piece the way
`../projects/<name>/` is. One folder per round, numbered, each with a `NOTES.md`
describing what changed and why. Not the model-tier pipeline (Digest/Plan/Execute/
Inspect) — these are direct small edits per Nathan's own workflow preference for this
kind of speed-focused tweak.
