Press by press

    Notes stacked in one row are pressed together. C4 is a white key, C#4 a black one — the dark chips are the black keys. Tap any row to hear it and jump there. If your app labels middle C as C3 instead of C4, switch Middle C is and every label shifts to match. Rejoin split notes repairs Basic Pitch's habit of breaking one held note into two consecutive ones. Kyutai does not do that, so it is off for this transcription — its zero-gap repeats are real.
