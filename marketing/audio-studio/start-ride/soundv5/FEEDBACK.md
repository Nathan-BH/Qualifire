## What this is

Same bass + voice-A layers as soundv4, but now scoped to just the ride:

- **Silent 0.0-5.3s** — black hold, reveal, overview map, START button, cursor press,
  camera push-in. Per `../../../silent-studio/start-ride/rounds/v4/FEEDBACK.md`'s beat
  list, the actual ride ("route draws behind the rider at constant speed") starts 5.3s.
- **Music 5.3-13.8s** — bass + voice A, restarted from its own beginning at 5.3s (not
  the mid-piece fragment that would land there if left at soundv4's original alignment),
  so the ride hears the theme's actual opening, not an arbitrary slice. Only the first
  8.5s of the 15.04s composition fits this window — 0.15s fade-in at 5.3s, 0.4s fade-out
  approaching 13.8s so it doesn't cut off mid-note.
- **Silent 13.8-14.0s** — the finish hold.

## What to listen for

- Whether starting the theme from its own beginning right as the ride begins (rather
  than wherever it happened to be) reads as intentional.
- Whether 8.5s is enough of the theme to register, or it feels cut short (the ride
  segment is shorter than the full clean phrase, so this can't play all of it).
- Whether silence during the button-press/camera-push section reads as "holding back
  before it matters" or just empty.
