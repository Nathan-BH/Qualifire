# BRAND — what Qualifire is, and how the design should say it

Author: Designer · 2026-08-15 · Status: PROPOSED (Nathan requested storytelling before logo concepts)
Feeds: logo concepts (`product/brand/`), two-mode theming (Nathan's "race mode" idea), D-013 palette.

## The story

Qualifire turns a fixed daily commute into a qualifying lap. Not racing other
people (D-010: never compare different roads, never other riders), not fitness
(no calories, no streaks, no averages — D-013's history screen is deliberately
"mostly flat"). One rider, three known tracks, and the only opponent is the
rolling 7-day and 28-day versions of yourself.

The core emotional loop the design must serve:

1. **The ritual.** Same road every morning. Press START at the door; the lap
   begins 162 m later, at speed, at a line only the app can see. The ride is
   unchanged — the *meaning* of the ride is new.
2. **Earned colour.** Colour is the scarcest resource in the app. A sector is
   yellow just for happening; green only when it beats your week; purple only
   when it beats your month — beyond a measured noise floor, so a colour can
   never be luck-of-the-GPS. Purple is rare *by design*. The app's restraint is
   what makes the purple moment worth chasing.
3. **No failure state.** A slow lap is "time posted". A red light is "‖", not
   a scolding. The app never punishes; it only occasionally celebrates.
   **Amended by Nathan 2026-08-15:** D-013's "no red" narrows to its real
   point — **red never encodes a timing state or feedback**, and never appears
   on the race surface. As *livery* (identity chrome in paddock mode) red is
   allowed and welcome: F1's own identity is red/white while its timing tower
   still speaks purple/green/yellow. Pending formal D-013 amendment at next
   cycle close.
4. **The instrument, not the coach.** While moving, the app is an inert timing
   instrument: zero touch targets, sound carries the story (D-019's E-major
   earcons), one glance maximum (D-006). It behaves like a pit wall, not a
   personal trainer.

**The name.** Quali + fire: the qualifying lap, and the rare moment a sector
lights up. The "fire" is not aggression — it is the lit-up timing chip.

## Design principles derived

**P1 — Two states: paddock and race** (Nathan's direction, 2026-08-15).
The app lives in two moods:
- **Paddock** (home, rides, history, setup, board review): warmer, livelier —
  charcoal `#17171b`-family background (the mockup's page chrome, easier on
  OLED than pure black), warm ink, the signature yellow allowed in chrome
  (active tab, START border, section accents).
- **Race** (live screen, and Record while recording): drops to near-black
  `#0A0A0A`, chrome recedes, tier colours become the only colour on screen —
  maximum contrast where a glance must land in under a second.
The transition is automatic (recording starts → race mode); no toggle to
fiddle with on the move. Manual override can come later if wanted.

**P2 — Colour hierarchy is meaning hierarchy.** Tier colours (purple > green >
yellow) always outrank identity colour. The signature yellow doubles as the
neutral tier — deliberate: the brand colour is the "time posted" colour, the
app's default temperature. Purple/green appear in chrome *never*; they are
earned surfaces only. Grey stays reserved for no-data.

**P3 — Numbers are the hero.** Heavy (800) tabular numerals at display sizes;
labels are small, uppercase, letterspaced servants. Any screen should be
readable as a timing tower from arm's length.

**P4 — The gate is the mark.** The one proprietary visual idea Qualifire owns
is the *gate line crossed at speed* — a chainage value, drawn perpendicular
across a path. The logo should come from this (or from the sector strip /
lap ring), not from generic bike/speed clichés.

## Motion (Nathan 2026-08-15: "the mark is built for animation")

The mark's two parts are the app's two nouns — ring = lap, slash = gate — so
every animation is a sentence, not decoration. Canonical uses (storyboard:
`brand/board_12_motion.png`): **app launch** — ring draws clockwise from the
start tick, slash lands last; **START pressed** — the yellow slab collapses
into the slash striking the ring (gates armed), screen drops to race black;
**tab switch** — the active-tab bar *is* the slash, sliding between tabs with
a 45° tilt mid-flight; **final gate** — ring pulses once in the lap-tier
colour, board slides in. Hard rule: motion lives in paddock and transitions
only; while riding nothing moves except numbers (D-006). Implementation note:
all four are doable JS-only with RN's Animated (no rebuild).

## Logo brief (for the 5 concepts)

- Must read at launcher-icon size (48 dp, circular mask) and as an in-app
  wordmark companion.
- Palette: charcoal ground, warm ink, signature yellow; at most one concept
  may use the full tier trio.
- No red, no wings, no generic bicycles, no speedometer needles.
- Should encode at least one of: the gate line, the Q of quali, the four-slot
  sector strip, the lap ring, the flying-start.
