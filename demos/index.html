Qualifire — demo surfaces

Every user-testable HTML page lives here (D-020). Open them straight from disk — no server needed. Last updated 2026-08-16.
The two current surfaces are the race mockup and the way maps. Everything else is either an audition tool or an older data check kept for reference — labelled below so nothing here can quietly pass for the live design.

Race surface

current mockup.html — the whole app, four tabs

Rebuilt 16 Aug from real data. Ride: start flow (detected landmark or picker, destination, "9 rides found"), then a real Morning commute replayed at 25× with a moving dot on a live map, the ticking clock, 2.5 s sector flashes, the rotating status line and the slim STOP. Routes: your six landmarks with true radii and the 11 curated ways. Result: lap, timing tower against the real archive ghosts, sector table. Settings: every toggle live.

Try this first: Settings → Colour model, switch between best / average / hybrid, then look at Result. Same ride, three different verdicts — that is the §19 question made concrete rather than argued.

Also switchable: red lights (auto-pause · manual button · off — the button appears on the ride screen and holds the clock), start place (detect vs choose), live map on/off, tower on/off, day/night.

Regenerate after any data change with python3 data/analysis/07_build_mockup.py. The cycle-007 design is preserved as legacy-mockup-cycle007.html.

new · 16 Aug tower-ghosts.html — where your lap would place, against real ghosts

The archive pre-seed made real: the last 10 rides per route (Morning · Evening A · Evening B), recomputed by our own pipeline from raw lat/lon/t — never Strava's numbers — and ranked by D-028. Drag the slider to see any lap time slot into the tower, with the gap to pole.

This is what Monday gives you: a real position instead of a null chip. Ghost rows are dimmed and stay demotable while D-024's tripwire is unratified. Curated-out rides (roadworks routes, the 2.3 km GPS jump) are excluded from the seed, so nothing you have to beat is a bad trace.

audition earcons-audition.html — the three tier sounds + lap voice

Plays each earcon in isolation (D-019) plus the two-part final-gate lap sound.

Open question (B-27): play these on the phone speaker outdoors at ~25 km/h — the lap voice sits at 330–494 Hz, below the wind-safe band, and nobody has tested it against real wind yet.

Where you ride

current ways/index.html — every way between your six landmarks

The 6×6 matrix of start→end pairs, linking to one map per way: 13 pages drawing the real GPX tracks on OpenStreetMap, teal one direction and magenta the other, landmark circles at their true radii. Rides are numbered, and your curation verdicts are rendered — ignored rides are struck through, grey-dashed and hidden by default, so nothing is deleted, only set aside.

Worth checking: the four station↔work rides and one home↔station ride still marked unreviewed; and whether "via fosh" should be one way or two, since out and back use different roads.

data check · Aug 14 routes-check.html — the original three-track clustering

How Morning / Evening A / Evening B were first separated out of the archive (D-015). Superseded as a browsing tool by the way maps, kept because it is the evidence behind the three-track decision.

data check · Aug 14 gates-check.html — the measured gate positions

Where the four sector boundaries sit on each track (D-016), with the speed and stop-fraction reasoning that placed them.

Still open (§22): the START gate sits ~160 m out for GPS-arming reasons. How much closer it can safely go is the next thing to measure — and moving a start or finish gate is the one gate change that breaks lap history, so it is worth doing once, deliberately.
