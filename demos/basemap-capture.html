Qualifire — real basemap capture

Fetches raster map tiles at zoom 15 and composites one 900×1400 basemap per ratified route, aligned exactly to the Web-Mercator transform already stored in app/assets/routes/routes.json. 63 unique tiles per route, one-off. The raw crop is saved as <Route>-base.png; all styling (desaturation, wash, route line, gates) is applied afterwards by data/analysis/08_build_route_assets.py, which stays the canonical renderer.

Not openstreetmap.org. Their tile usage policy forbids scripted/bulk fetching and they block it — correctly. Every provider below is one that permits this use. Attribution is required and is rendered by the app, not baked into the asset (a moving window would crop a baked corner off).
